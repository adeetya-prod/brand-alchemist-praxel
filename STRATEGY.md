# Brand Alchemist Strategy

## Target problem

Solo founders and small business owners can't afford a designer but still need consistent,
on-brand visuals across every channel. Without a system that enforces brand rules, every
new creative starts from scratch — and the result looks scrappy or off-brand.

## Our approach

Brand rules first, generation second: we read the actual brand identity — colours, fonts,
tone, logo — before any creative is made, so every output is constrained by the brand, not
just prompted by text. Unlike generic AI image tools or Canva, Brand Alchemist enforces
brand consistency automatically, so users look professional without a designer in the loop.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Solo founders and small business owners — they're hiring Brand Alchemist to
produce consistent, on-brand social media creatives without a designer, a budget, or hours
of manual work.

## Key metrics

- **Week-2 retention** — % of new sign-ups who generate at least one creative in week 2; measured in the database.
- **Creatives generated per active user / week** — leading signal of product stickiness; drops if output quality or UX regresses; measured in DB.
- **MRR (paying subscribers)** — lagging proof of value; measured in Stripe.

## Tracks

### Billing & paywall

Add Stripe subscriptions with a free tier (limited generations) and a paid tier that
unlocks full usage. The monetisation gate without which no revenue is possible.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Brand rules enforcement is the value — the paywall lets us
capture that value once users experience it in the free tier.

### Brand quality & output fidelity

Continuously improve the accuracy with which generated creatives reflect actual brand
identity: colours applied correctly, layout proportions respected, tone matched.

_Why it serves the approach:_ "Brand rules first" only works if the rules are actually
visible in the output. Low fidelity kills retention before the paywall even matters.

### Onboarding & first creative

Reduce time-to-first-on-brand-creative. Solo founders have low patience — if they don't
see a finished, usable asset in session one, they don't return.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Brand consistency is hard to believe until you see it. The
first creative is the proof point that earns everything downstream.
